## How to use

1. In **Google Flow**, choose to create an **image**, not a video.
2. Set the **aspect ratio** from the table before generating.
3. Paste the whole grey prompt block. Each block is complete on its own.
4. Generate a few variations and pick one using the **Check before you keep it** list.
5. Download at the largest size Flow offers (2K or 4K), as JPG.
6. Save it with the exact filename above, or send it to me and I'll place it.

**Consistency:** you already have Prompt 2's photo. If Flow lets you add a reference image, attach `section-1-problem.jpg` when you generate Prompts 1 and 3 so the towers, mountains and color grade match.

## Prompt 1 · Title hero
**Save as:** `figures/flow/title-hero.jpg` · **3:4 portrait, 1620 × 2160 px or larger**

```
FORMAT: Photorealistic photograph, 3:4 portrait orientation, 1620 x 2160 pixels or larger. Sharp at full resolution.

SUBJECT: A small autonomous quadcopter drone hovers in mid-air about one meter in front of a vertical hydroponic lettuce tower, its camera pointed straight at the plants as if scanning them. The drone is small and lightweight, about 15 centimeters wide, roughly the size of a dinner plate: an X-shaped matte black carbon-fiber frame with exactly four arms and exactly four two-blade propellers, a small camera lens on the front, a short thin GPS mast standing up from the top, and a tiny green circuit board visible between the arms. The propellers are slightly motion-blurred to show it is flying. No brand markings.

THE TOWER: One clean white PVC pipe column, about 10 centimeters wide and 1.8 meters tall, standing straight up on a round dark-green plastic reservoir base. Short white planting cups stick out of the pipe at a 45-degree upward angle, arranged in neat staggered rows all around the column from bottom to top. About 85 percent of the cups hold healthy, bright green butterhead lettuce heads with dew on the leaves; the rest are empty, showing a dark opening. Small black-and-white square marker stickers, each the size of a palm, are stuck flat on the pipe between some cups. A small grey weatherproof box with a tiny blank screen is mounted on the pipe just above the base. The whole tower is visible from base to top with space above it. No metal frames, no trellis, no rust.

SETTING: A small outdoor hydroponic lettuce farm in the highlands of Nueva Vizcaya, northern Philippines. A few more identical towers stand far behind, blurred. Layers of misty green mountains in the far background.

LIGHT: Early golden hour just after sunrise. Warm low sunlight from the right side creates a soft golden rim light on the drone and the edges of the lettuce leaves. Light haze in the air. The sky is soft pale gold, not blown-out white.

CAMERA AND COMPOSITION: Eye level with the drone, about 2 meters from the tower. 50mm lens at f/2. The tower runs vertically through the center of the frame; the drone floats at mid-height on the right side of the tower, facing it. The drone and the nearest lettuce are in sharp focus; the background melts into soft bokeh.

STYLE: Premium editorial magazine photograph, natural light, true-to-life colors, subtle film grain. Color palette: deep forest green, fresh leaf green, warm golden light, soft cream highlights.

AVOID: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, brand names, readable QR codes, more or fewer than four propellers, propeller guards, a large photography drone, sci-fi glow, laser beams, holograms, people, rust, metal scaffolding around the tower, cropped top of the tower.
```

**Check before you keep it**
- [ ] The drone has exactly 4 arms and 4 propellers, and it's smaller than a lettuce-tower section.
- [ ] The whole tower is visible from base to top.
- [ ] There is no readable text or logo anywhere.
- [ ] The sky is warm, not plain white.
