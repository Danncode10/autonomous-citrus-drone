What we will build, and what we leave out

Scope

Included
  • Tower registration by QR code from an ESP32 tower unit with GPS and an e-paper display
  • Autonomous scan: GPS to the tower, YOLOv8 + QR + ArUco alignment, every face bottom to top
  • Live detection of lettuce and empty pots on the ground laptop, matched by face, level and position
  • Offline cache and dashboard: SQLite cache, farmer-initiated upload to Supabase, Next.js web dashboard
  • Evaluation: detection, counting, slot matching, alignment, scan success, streaming, flight time, and user ratings

Delimitations

Excluded or limited
  • Prototype only: a structural frame, with no working watering or nutrient system
  • Lettuce only, classified as occupied or empty; no disease, pest, growth-stage or weight assessment
  • Open outdoor area with GPS: no obstacle avoidance, indoor or GPS-denied flight; 2D RGB video only, no LiDAR
  • Within Wi-Fi range ([Wi-Fi range, m]); about 3–5 minutes of flight per battery, one tower per flight
  • Fair weather only: wind ≤ [max wind, km/h]; no rain or night flights; no cellular, cloud GPU or commercial deployment
