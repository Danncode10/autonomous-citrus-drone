From a QR code on the tower to a count on the dashboard

01Offline

Tower unit

Farmer presses Generate; the ESP32 averages its GPS and shows a QR with tower ID, coordinates and checksum on e-paper.

02Online

Register and request

The farmer scans the QR with a phone, registers the tower in Supabase and requests a scan.

03Offline

Fly to the tower

The ground laptop sends the mission; the drone flies by GPS waypoint to an approach point.

04Offline

Align and scan

YOLOv8 finds the tower; the Pi uses the QR and ArUco markers to hold distance, height and face, scanning each face bottom to top.

05Offline

Detect and match

Live video over local Wi-Fi to the ground laptop; YOLOv8 finds lettuce and empty pots and matches each to its pot. Saved in the offline cache.

06Online

Sync

When the farmer chooses and internet is available, the Sync Center uploads the results to Supabase.

07Online

View results

The Next.js dashboard shows lettuce and empty pot counts per tower on a phone or computer.
