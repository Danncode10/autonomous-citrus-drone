What this study sets out to do

General objective

To design and develop an autonomous drone-based monitoring system that registers vertical hydroponic towers through a location-based QR code, navigates to each tower using GPS, aligns itself using computer vision and visual markers, scans every face of the tower in an open space, detects lettuce plants and empty planting pots using computer vision, and displays tower-level records on a web-based dashboard.

Specific objectives RAD phase where each objective is achieved
  1. 01

    Baseline requirements

    Identify the existing tools, processes and challenges in monitoring vertical hydroponic farming to establish the system's baseline requirements.

    Requirements Planning
  2. 02

    Tower unit & web dashboard

    Design and develop a tower unit that generates a location-based QR code, and a web dashboard for QR-based tower registration, scan requests and inspection records.

    Design · Construction
  3. 03

    Navigation & alignment

    Develop an autonomous navigation and alignment method that combines GPS waypoints, YOLOv8 tower detection, and QR code and ArUco marker positioning to scan every face of a tower.

    Rapid Construction
  4. 04

    Detection & integration

    Develop a YOLOv8 model that detects lettuce heads and empty pots from live aerial video, matches each to its exact pot, and integrates it with drone telemetry, the offline cache and the web dashboard.

    Rapid Construction
  5. 05

    Evaluation

    Evaluate the system in terms of detection and counting accuracy, alignment and scan success, and its usefulness, ease of use and user satisfaction.

    Cutover
