From approval to evaluation, in five steps

  1. Step 1

    Secure approval

    • Questionnaire submitted to the adviser for suggestions and approval
    • Letter of request; approvals from school authorities and farm owners
  2. Step 2

    Interview farmers

    • Face-to-face, about 15 minutes each
    • Purpose explained; informed consent obtained
    • Audio recorded only with permission
  3. Step 3

    Observe the site

    • Fill in the site observation sheet
    • Photograph the towers, without people
    • Add tower images to the Supabox dataset
  4. Step 4

    Test the system

    • After development, run test flights and detection tests in the testing area
    • Record the results
  5. Step 5

    User evaluation

    • Respondents are shown how to use the system
    • They answer the USE Questionnaire
Ethics
Voluntary. Respondents may skip questions or stop at any time
Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173); farms stay anonymous unless they agree
CAAP PCAR Part 11. Supervised flights, 30 m from non-participants, RC override
AI tools. Outputs reviewed by researchers; no personal data shared
