Towers grow upward. Pot checks are still manual.

The problem

Farms are going vertical

Hydroponic towers stack crops in tiers to save land, and every tier adds pots to check. (Despommier, 2010)

Monitoring is still by hand

A lettuce farm in Iloilo recorded its data manually, with inconsistent results. (Domingo, 2022)

Sensors can't see empty pots

IoT sensors track temperature, pH and nutrients, but cannot tell a planted pot from an empty one. (Chowdhury et al., 2020)

Our approach

A small drone flies to each registered tower, scans every face, bottom to top, and YOLOv8 counts lettuce heads and empty pots.

  1. 01

    QR code tower registration

    Tower unit shows a QR with the tower ID and GPS location

  2. 02

    GPS + ArUco marker alignment

    GPS reaches the tower; markers hold distance, height and face

  3. 03

    Live YOLOv8 detection

    Ground laptop maps each lettuce and empty pot to its slot

  4. 04

    Offline-first web dashboard

    Works on local Wi-Fi; syncs when internet is available

Why it matters: slot availability for every tower without manual walk-throughs, at low cost, with no internet needed at the farm.
