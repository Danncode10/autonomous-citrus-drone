Who benefits, and how

Practical

Hydroponic Farmers

Less time checking towers pot by pot, especially the upper levels. Updated counts guide replanting, harvesting and space use.

Practical

Farm Managers and Workers

A clear per-tower record of occupancy and inspection history, with fewer counting errors.

Technical

Technical Contribution

Extends marker-based drone alignment from landing pads to the vertical faces of a tower, and maps each detection to its exact slot.

Academic

Students and Researchers

A reference for drone-based crop monitoring. The labeled dataset can support future plant health, pest or harvest-readiness studies.

Academic

Academic Community

Adds to knowledge on smart farming, robotics and applied computer vision in local agriculture.

Community

Local Community and Agricultural Offices

Guides the promotion of affordable urban and vertical farming technology for food security.
