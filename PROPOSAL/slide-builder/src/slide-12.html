Quantitative · system tests

Performance measures

Detection model
  • Precision, recall, mAP@50
  • Counting accuracy against manual counts of lettuce and empty pots
Flight tests
  • Successful alignment rate; mean alignment error (cm)
  • Completed full-tower scan rate; slot matching accuracy
  • Stream delay and frame loss; flight time per tower
Quantitative · USE Questionnaire

Weighted mean and standard deviation

Computed for each category: usefulness, ease of use and user satisfaction. The mean shows the average rating; the SD shows how similar the responses are.
