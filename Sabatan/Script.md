# GrowKita Pitch Script

Keep the delivery conversational. Do not read every word exactly; use each section as your main message for the slide.

## Slide 1 - GrowKita

Good day, judges. We are GrowKita, an AI-powered farm intelligence platform. We help farms see what is happening in their fields earlier, so they can make better harvest and sales decisions. We begin with citrus yield estimation in Nueva Vizcaya.

## Slide 2 - Our Vision

Our goal is to make AI practical for Philippine agriculture. We combine drone imagery, computer vision, and farm data, then turn them into insights a farmer can actually use. We are building toward one monitoring platform, but we begin with one clear use case.

## Slide 3 - Why Citrus

We start with citrus because Nueva Vizcaya gives us a strong local advantage. It lets us build a focused Perante orange dataset, work closely with nearby farms, and prove that useful yield information has real value. Focus first; expansion follows proof.

## Slide 4 - The Problem

Before harvest, farms need to answer three business questions: how much fruit to expect, how many workers to prepare, and what volume they can commit to buyers. Today, counting is manual, slow, and inconsistent across many trees.

## Slide 5 - The Solution

GrowKita turns a guided farm scan into a clearer estimate. The drone captures the farm, the software detects visible fruit, and the report supports harvest and sales planning. The drone is the tool, the software is the brain, and the report is the value.

## Slide 6 - What the Farm Receives

The farm sees an estimated visible fruit count, a map by farm block, areas that need a manual check, and scan history over time. We are careful with our claim: this is a visible fruit-count estimate, not a promise of the final harvest. Accuracy and trust come first.

## Slide 7 - How We Make Money

GrowKita has two clear ways to earn. Larger customers can buy the drone-and-software package, including training and support. Farms that are not ready to buy the system can pay per hectare for GrowKita to scan their farm and deliver the report. Our starting estimates are around PHP 100,000 to PHP 150,000 for the system, and PHP 5,000 to PHP 10,000 per hectare for a survey scan. These are not final prices; we will validate them with experts, prototype cost, and real customer feedback.

## Slide 8 - Why We Can Win

The drone alone is not our advantage. Our advantage is the local system around it: crop-specific data, an improving AI model, repeat scan history, a practical field workflow, training, and trusted partners in Nueva Vizcaya. That is much harder to copy than hardware alone.

## Slide 9 - The Data Flywheel

With clear farm permission, every quality-checked scan helps improve the citrus model. Better models create better reports, better reports build trust, and trust brings repeat customers. Farm-identifiable data stays protected; any aggregate insight must be consent-based.

## Slide 10 - Growth Path

Our plan is disciplined. First, prove that farms will pay per hectare for useful citrus scans and that larger buyers will consider the full system package. Next, improve reliability and training. Only after that do we run paid pilots for the next crop or controlled environment.

## Slide 11 - Future Applications

Once the workflow works for citrus, the same platform can be tested in hydroponics, other trees and crops, animal facilities, and research systems. The shared value is simple: observe, organize data, analyze with AI, and give the customer a useful decision. These are future pilots, not promises; each one must earn its place with a paying customer.

## Slide 12 - Pilot Plan and Ask

Our next milestone is not a large rollout. It is a strong local proof point: a citrus farm that uses our report to make a better decision, plus a serious buyer who can evaluate the full drone-and-software package. We are seeking pilot farms, cooperatives, agricultural mentors, and support for field testing and model validation.

## Slide 13 - Closing / Team

GrowKita starts with a local citrus problem, but it is designed to become a trusted farm intelligence platform. We are ready to build the first version, validate it with real farms, and grow only where we can create measurable value. Thank you.
