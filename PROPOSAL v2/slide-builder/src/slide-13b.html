How the drone scans a tower, and what if it fails

  1. 1

    GPS waypoint

    Laptop sends the mission; drone flies to an approach point

  2. 2

    Search Pi

    Turns slowly until it sees the 15 × 15 cm approach marker

  3. 3

    Align Pi

    6 × 6 cm face markers → solvePnP → small MAVLink corrections

  4. 4

    Climb face A

    Bottom to top; markers give each pot's level and position

  5. 5

    Next face, repeat

    Moves around the tower until every face is covered

  6. 6

    Land and finalize

    1080p recording copied to the laptop for the final count

Failsafes
Wi-Fi to the laptop drops
Keeps scanning. Final counts come from the onboard recording.
Markers are lost
Holds ~10 s, then returns to launch if not found.
RC link or battery lost
ArduPilot failsafe returns to launch.
Wind rises past the limit
Operator cancels; drone returns to launch.
The RC transmitter stays with a trained researcher as a manual override.
