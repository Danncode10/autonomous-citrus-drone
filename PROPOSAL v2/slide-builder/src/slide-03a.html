Farms are going vertical. Monitoring hasn't caught up.

01 · Vertical farming

Towers grow more on less land

Vertical farms save land and water, but only when they are managed well. Local towers are built from separate slots.

EvidenceTowers need good monitoring

02 · Manual monitoring

Farms still check by hand

Farms in Iloilo and Bohol still log their towers by hand. Knowing where the plants are helps plan the space.

EvidenceMonitoring is still manual
Domingo (2022) · Bunga (2026) · Petropoulou et al. (2023)
03 · IoT and smart farming

Sensors watch the water, not the pots
