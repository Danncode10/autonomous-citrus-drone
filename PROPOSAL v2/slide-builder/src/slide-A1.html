Strengths, weaknesses, and the risks we plan for

Strengths

Internal
  • Automates counting of lettuce and empty pots
  • Low-cost drone and 2D camera, no LiDAR
  • Scans every face and identifies each pot
  • Inspection history and a live scan preview
  • Works offline; upload later
  • Towers need only a printed sign

Weaknesses

Internal
  • Lettuce only
  • Short flight time, small batteries
  • Needs GPS and an open space
  • Live preview needs Wi-Fi range
  • Accuracy depends on the dataset
  • Pots with no visible marker matched with lower confidence

Opportunities

External
  • Saved images reused for health and harvest studies
  • Other crops and farm types
  • Cellular and cloud processing for large farms
  • Work with local farms and agri offices

Threats

External
  • Strong wind and rain
  • GPS drift causing misalignment
  • Weak Wi-Fi interrupting the live stream
  • Lighting changes lowering accuracy
