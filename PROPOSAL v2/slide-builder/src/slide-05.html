What this study sets out to do

General objective

Design and develop an autonomous drone-based monitoring system that registers towers through a printed QR code and the farmer's phone location, navigates by GPS, aligns using computer vision and visual markers, scans every face in an open space, detects lettuce and empty pots, and shows tower-level records on a web dashboard.
