From approval to evaluation, in five steps

STEP 1

Secure Approval

  • Letter of request
  • Approval from school authorities and the farm owner
STEP 2

Validation of Instruments

  • Interview questionnaire reviewed and approved by the adviser
STEP 3

Data Collection

  • ~15 min interviews with informed consent
  • Site observation sheet
  • Tower photos into the Supabox dataset
  • Test flights, then the USE Questionnaire
STEP 4

Data Processing

  • Code interview responses
  • Log test results per tower and flight
  • Tally USE ratings per category
STEP 5

Data Analysis

  • Themes update the SWOT
  • Performance metrics
  • Weighted mean and SD
