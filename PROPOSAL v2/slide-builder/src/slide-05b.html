Specific objectives

1

To identify the existing tools, process, and challenges in monitoring vertical hydroponic farming to establish the system's baseline requirements.

2

To design and develop a web dashboard for QR-based tower registration using the location of the farmer's phone, scan requests, and inspection records that works even when the farm has no internet connection.

3

To develop an autonomous navigation and alignment method that combines GPS waypoints with onboard QR code and ArUco marker positioning to scan every face of a tower.

4

To develop a YOLOv8 computer vision model that detects lettuce heads and empty planting pots from the aerial video, shown as a live preview during flight and finalized from the full onboard recording after landing, and matches each detection to its exact pot on the tower, and to integrate it with the drone telemetry, offline cache, and web dashboard into a unified crop tracking system.

5

To evaluate the system in terms of detection and counting accuracy, alignment and scan success, and its usefulness, ease of use, and user satisfaction.
